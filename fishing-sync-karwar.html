The sync sequence

From open sea to harbour ledger, in three handoffs.

Each boat carries the same three steps every trip. The order matters — it's the order data actually moves.

01 — At sea

Log the catch

Crew records species, approximate weight, and GPS position on the shared log, even with patchy signal — it queues and sends once back in range.

02 — Approaching harbour

Sync to the board

As the boat nears Karwar port, its entry appears on the live harbour board so unloading crews and buyers know what's coming in.

03 — At the quay

Settle at today's rate

The catch is weighed against the logged estimate and sold at the day's posted rate — no separate phone calls to confirm prices.
